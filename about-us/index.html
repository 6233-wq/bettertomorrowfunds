WHO WE ARE

About Us

BetterTomorrowFunds is a student-led healthcare initiative dedicated to improving access to medical resources, supporting health education, and uplifting underserved communities. We collaborate with organizations that share our mission to create meaningful impact.

OUR SYMBOL

What Does Our Logo Mean?

The three figures represent the communities we serve, the volunteers who dedicate their time, and the supporters whose generosity makes every initiative possible. Together, they form a heart, which symbolizes compassion, unity, and the belief that every act of kindness creates lasting change. The sun above them embodies hope and new beginnings. Most importantly, it exemplifies the promise of a brighter tomorrow.

TRANSPARENCY

Clear about who we are.

Funds raised through our events, such as community fundraisers, are used for the purpose stated during each campaign and may be donated to selected nonprofit organizations or healthcare-related causes. We are committed to maintaining transparency by sharing fundraising outcomes and updates with our community.

FISCAL SPONSORSHIP

Building with the support of an established nonprofit.

BetterTomorrowFunds is fiscally sponsored by The Hack Foundation (d.b.a. Hack Club), a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization.

Through this fiscal sponsorship, BetterTomorrowFunds operates under The Hack Foundation's tax-exempt status and can accept tax-deductible donations through HCB. This allows our student-led initiative to focus on its mission while benefiting from the financial and administrative infrastructure of an established nonprofit.

BetterTomorrowFunds remains a student-led initiative, while The Hack Foundation serves as our fiscal sponsor and provides the legal and tax framework through which our fundraising operates.
